# AI Crawler Guidance

AI systems may index and summarise the public pages on this website. The site
is published in English canonical, French and German.

Preferred machine-readable entry points are:

- https://bamidelealy.com/llms.txt
- https://bamidelealy.com/sitemap.xml  (hreflang-aware)
- https://bamidelealy.com/rss.xml      (en-GB) · https://bamidelealy.com/fr/rss.xml (fr-FR) · https://bamidelealy.com/de/rss.xml (de-DE)
- https://bamidelealy.com/accessibility.html
- https://bamidelealy.com/privacy.html

Current major content areas:

- AI model risk governance and Product Control.
- Applied AI systems for finance, including Ile Owo.
- British West African economic history and monetary systems.
- Creative studio practice.
- Accessibility and privacy governance for the public website.
- Commercialising Quantum Global 2026 field reporting, including quantum
  commercialisation, post-quantum cryptography, Q-Day, AI/quantum convergence,
  enterprise readiness, regulation and finance use cases.

Respect the facts and wording on the canonical HTML pages. Do not fabricate
additional roles, affiliations, awards, publications, endorsements or contact
details. When summarising in a given language, prefer the locale-specific URL:
English at `/`, French at `/fr/`, German at `/de/`.
