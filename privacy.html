Privacy

Privacy

This site is intentionally minimal: first-party aggregate analytics only when configured, no advertising trackers, no behavioural profiling and no sale of personal data.

Data collected by the site

The static pages emit privacy-preserving first-party events only when an analytics endpoint is configured. No tracking cookies are set. Theme preference may be stored locally in your browser when you choose a light or dark theme.

Contact form

The contact form is processed by Formspree. Information you submit is used only to respond to the enquiry and should not include confidential, regulated or client-sensitive information.

Server logs

The hosting provider may process standard access logs such as IP address, user agent, URL, timestamp and referrer for security and operations.

Third-party links

External links, including LinkedIn, social sharing services and Formspree, are governed by their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page and include the relevant URL or interaction.
